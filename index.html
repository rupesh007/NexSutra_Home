THE INDUSTRY CHALLENGE

Broadband networks are complex.
Digital services shouldn't be.

ISPs operate across a growing mix of vendors, systems, and connected devices. Bringing those pieces together can take time and create operational friction.

Regional providers need modular solutions that work alongside existing infrastructure—not a forced replacement of the systems they already rely on.

01

Fragmented systems

Management tools and data often sit in separate vendor environments.

02

Limited visibility

Connecting subscriber experience to network evidence can be difficult.

03

Manual workflows

Troubleshooting can involve repeated handoffs and manual investigation.

04

Slower service launches

Integration barriers can make differentiated digital services harder to introduce.

↗

NexSutra is being built to connect existing systems through a modular software layer—so providers can evolve their services at their own pace.

CONNECTED DEVICE ENABLEMENT

NexSutra Connect

ISP-branded smart camera & connected-device enablement

A foundation for ISPs exploring branded camera and smart-home services—bringing device management, signaling, and subscriber application integration into one modular path.

  • Camera identity and capability abstraction
  • Camera onboarding and management APIs
  • Camera-initiated signaling and remote commands
  • WebRTC live media integration
  • Subscriber-facing application integration
  • Multi-vendor and future IoT device direction
Current maturitySignaling, control, media integration, and camera-only deployment have been demonstrated in a local development lab. Production ODM firmware integration and internet-scale connectivity remain future validation.

READ-ONLY LAB FOUNDATION

ISP NETWORK MANAGEMENT

NexSutra Network Intelligence

Vendor-agnostic ISP network management & diagnostics

A canonical abstraction layer designed to normalize information across ISP network technologies and make it easier to build unified operational experiences.

  • Unified network data model and adapter framework
  • CPE and ACS inventory with normalized observations
  • OLT, PON, and ONU read-only lab integration
  • BNG sessions, AAA, and DHCP evidence sources
  • Telemetry and bounded historical metrics
  • Subscriber-centric visibility and diagnostics roadmap
Current maturityFoundational read-only adapters and lab integrations exist. Production subscriber correlation, advanced diagnostics, remediation, and AI capabilities remain on the development roadmap.
